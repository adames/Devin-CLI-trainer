Migrate workflow files into skills and remove the originals

Usage: devin migrate workflows [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --scope <SCOPE>
          Which workflows to migrate: all (default), workspace, or global

          Possible values:
          - all:       Migrate both workspace and global workflows
          - workspace: Migrate only workspace-level workflows
          - global:    Migrate only global workflows from ~/.codeium/
          
          [default: all]

  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')
