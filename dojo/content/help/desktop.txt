Open Devin Desktop

Launches the Devin Desktop app matching this CLI's channel (e.g. `devin-insiders desktop` opens Devin Desktop Insiders), forwarding any extra arguments to its launcher.

Usage: devin desktop [ARGS]...

Arguments:
  [ARGS]...
          Arguments forwarded to the Devin Desktop launcher (e.g. paths)

Options:
  -h, --help
          Print help (see a summary with '-h')
